# ADR-0003: UUID identifiers

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Context

IDs appear in URLs (`/accounts/{id}`). Sequential numbers reveal business volume (how many customers) and make guessing other resources easy.

## Decision

All primary keys are UUIDs generated by the application. Human-facing identifiers (IBAN, customer number, transaction reference) are separate unique business keys.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| `BIGSERIAL` | Smaller, ordered | Enumerable, leaks volume |
| `BIGSERIAL` + public UUID column | Small FKs | Two IDs per entity, more complexity |

## Consequences

Slightly larger indexes (irrelevant at our scale). IDs are known before insert, which simplifies building ledger entries and tests. Time-ordered UUIDs (v7) may be adopted later without API change.
